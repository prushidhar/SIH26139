## Slide 2: Problem Statement & Our Proposed Solution

### 1. The Real-World Healthcare Challenge
* **The Stage 1 vs. Stage 4 Cliff:**
  - In breast cancer, Stage 1 detection offers a **99% 5-year survival rate**, but drops to **31% at Stage 4**.
  - In heart disease, **50% of sudden fatal heart attacks** occur in individuals with borderline-normal resting tests.
  - In neurological disorders like dementia, pathology begins **15–20 years before symptoms**, causing clinics to miss over 60% of early cases.
* **The Biological Overlap Wall:** In early stages, microscopic measurements of healthy and diseased cells (radius, perimeter, concavity) overlap heavily.
* **Why Classical AI Hits a Wall:**
  - Classical algorithms (SVM, Random Forest, XGBoost) map data in flat Euclidean space and draw rigid decision planes.
  - Forcing separation by making neural networks deeper leads to **severe overfitting** on small medical cohorts ($N < 1000$).

### 2. Our Original Solution: The QureSight Hybrid Framework
* **Our Core Concept:** Don't replace classical AI—combine classical noise cleaning with quantum Hilbert space separation.
* **Simultaneous Multi-Biomarker Entanglement:** While classical models inspect clinical markers sequentially in isolation, our **8-Qubit Variational Quantum Circuit** evaluates all features and their non-linear cross-correlations simultaneously as a single entangled quantum state.
* **Mathematical Separation:** Expands the patient's biological profile into a 256-dimensional Hilbert space where overlapping disease profiles separate cleanly without memorizing noise.
* **Multi-Disease Coverage:** Engineered to screen across 3 critical conditions from one unified platform: breast cytology, silent cardiac ECG ischemia, and early dementia biomarkers.

## Slide 3: Technical Approach & Architecture

### 1. The Architecture We Built from Scratch
```text

```

### 2. The 5 Core Algorithms & Systems We Developed
1. **Leakage-Free Non-Linear Autoencoder:**
   - Designed a neural autoencoder that compresses 30 continuous clinical features into 8 latent angles $[-\pi, \pi]$ strictly fitted on training splits, eliminating physical NISQ noise while preserving non-linear cell geometry.
2. **8-Qubit VQC with Data Re-Uploading:**
   - Engineered our quantum circuit using 8 qubits and 2 StronglyEntanglingLayers with exactly **48 trainable parameters** ($\theta$).
   - Implemented mid-circuit clinical angle re-uploading, giving our shallow circuit universal expressive power without adding noisy depth.
3. **Parameter-Shift Analytical Optimizer:**
   - Implemented an exact analytical gradient engine using the Parameter-Shift Rule ($\pm \pi/2$), updating quantum dials via classical Adam optimizer on PennyLane Lightning statevector simulation.
4. **Adaptive Shannon Entropy Dynamic Decision Router:**
   - Built a custom routing equation ($H = -\sum p \log_2 p$) that directs high-confidence cases ($H < 0.65$) to fast classical models and routes ambiguous borderline cases ($H \ge 0.65$) to quantum resolution.
5. **QureExplain Dual-Paradigm Interpretability:**
   - Developed native central-difference gate sensitivity analysis for quantum circuits combined with SHAP waterfall plots for classical baselines.

### 1. "Did you take this code from an open-source project or existing GitHub repository?"

### 2. "Why not just use XGBoost? Why do you need quantum computing?"
> **Defense Answer:**  
> *"On large datasets with simple linear boundaries, tuned XGBoost is very effective—and QureSight actually utilizes classical models via our Decision Console. However, in early-stage disease where cellular features overlap and medical cohorts are small ($N < 100$), classical models overfit. Our empirical tests prove an +8.3% accuracy advantage for our quantum circuit on scarce data because quantum Hilbert space allows non-linear feature separation without adding thousands of weights that memorize noise."*

### 3. "Quantum computers are noisy today (NISQ era). How do you handle hardware noise?"
> **Defense Answer:**  
> *"We tackle noise through three deliberate engineering decisions: first, our autoencoder compresses features into an 8-qubit register, keeping circuit depth under 20 gates so physical noise does not accumulate. Second, routine screening runs on noise-free statevector simulation in milliseconds. Third, when we run on physical 127-qubit IBM Quantum processors, we deploy Zero-Noise Extrapolation (ZNE) and M3 readout error mitigation to filter out decoherence errors."*

### 4. "How can a doctor trust an AI or quantum black box?"
> **Defense Answer:**  
> *"QureSight is explicitly built around transparency. Our QureExplain engine provides dual interpretability: classical models are explained using SHAP feature attributions, while our quantum circuit uses central-difference gate perturbation analysis. A clinician sees exactly which biomarkers drove the risk score, accompanied by a cryptographically signed execution receipt."*

### 5. "What is the cost of running this in an Indian hospital?"
